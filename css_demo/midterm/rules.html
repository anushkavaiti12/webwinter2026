Rules of Badminton

Badminton has a clear set of rules that help keep the game fair and competitive. Matches are played either as singles or doubles, and players must follow serving and scoring rules carefully.

  • A match is played as best of three games
  • Each game is played to 21 points
  • A point is scored on every serve
  • The birdie must land inside the court lines
  • A match can be played as singles or doubles
